Enterprise Suite · Deal to cash
Staff Product Designer · design direction

5 roles

touch every deal after signing. The customer sees none of it.

Five roles touch a deal before it becomes revenue. Our team worked on what they share.

The brief

Help sellers quote faster, and give customers a way to manage their own orders.

What was underneath

Once a deal is signed, the order passes through several teams and systems. The customer cannot see any of it, and each team holds only part of the picture.

What we did

  • Shaped a self serve order experience, so customers can see what they bought, what they will pay and what happens next, and resolve common issues without calling anyone.
  • Tested demand before committing, and took early designs to customers.
  • Designed with the other roles in mind: the order desk, customer success and implementation teams.
  • Explored AI assistance for sellers preparing quotes, with people keeping the final decision.

My role: experience strategy and design direction, working with two designers and partners across product, engineering and operations.

Intuit · Bills & Subscriptions
Staff Product Designer · FY26

4 questions

every customer asks of a bill: what do I owe, why did it change, what am I using, how do I pay?

Billing is where trust is earned or lost. The team set out to make the page explain itself.

The brief

Refresh the Bills & Subscriptions page.

What was underneath

The page listed what customers own but gave little help in understanding a charge. Their questions ended up in spreadsheets and support calls.

What we did

  • Reviewed the page through a heuristic evaluation, customer research and a comparison with how other billing pages work.
  • Made the case to leadership that the page should explain, not just display.
  • Set a direction built around a clear billing summary, usage and limits kept in view, and AI help that explains a charge where the customer is looking.
  • Kept it practical: it builds on existing platforms and is designed for price disclosure rules.

My role: staff designer, working with a principal designer and a PM, and contributing research and competitive analysis.

Amazon · Shopping app
UX Leader · 2022 to 2025

7 → 1

services, one app

Seven services sat behind one icon. With the team, we helped customers find them.

The brief

Make seven Amazon services feel like one app.

What was underneath

Customers knew the store names but not what each store was for. The stores blurred together, and most visits began on search or product pages, so a homepage fix alone would never reach them.

What we did

  • Worked on how customers discover stores: a consistent way for each store to show its value, with standardised motion that teams could adopt at scale.
  • Helped write the frameworks and mechanisms so other teams could carry the work across emerging markets without me in the room.
  • Contributed to discovery inside search and product pages, and to how colour and the app frame behave across the homepage and the pages after it.

My role: design lead for the experience, working with a Design Director, researchers, co designers and engineers.

Amazon India · Fresh
UX Leader · value perception

40 → 80

40→80%

cashback utilisation, with a 1.1% lift in conversion

Customers had the value in hand. They just couldn’t see it.

The brief

Help customers see the value of Fresh.

What was underneath

The value was there, but customers could not see it. They knew cashback existed, but not what it was worth or where to use it.

What we did

  • Made the value easier to understand at the moments when customers were deciding.
  • Cashback utilisation rose from 40% to 80%, and conversion grew by 1.1%.
  • The solution then scaled beyond Fresh.

My role: UX lead for the Fresh value experience, working with the Fresh team and partners.
