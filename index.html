Intuit Enterprise Suite · Deal to cash
Staff Product Designer · design direction

3 → 1

systems, becoming one deal flow

One deal crosses three systems before it becomes revenue.

The brief said

Design an AI quote assistant for sellers, and a self serve portal for their customers.

What was underneath

Sellers lost time stitching three systems together. And once a contract was signed, customers lost sight of their order: what happens now, and when.

What changes

One flow from lead to cash. For sellers, an agent drafts, checks and routes quotes, and they approve anything that matters. For customers, a self serve portal that answers “where is my order?” before they have to ask.

Experience strategy and design direction, with two designers.

Intuit QuickBooks · Bills & Subscriptions
Staff Product Designer · FY26

Trust hub

for every Intuit customer’s subscriptions

A transactional billing page, repositioned as a place customers trust.

The brief said

Make the billing page clearer.

What was underneath

The page was purely transactional. A detailed heuristic evaluation and the customer research made the real need plain: people wanted to understand what they pay for, and why.

What changes

Brought executives into alignment around that evidence, and repositioned the page as a trust hub for anyone with an Intuit subscription: AI driven insights, plain answers, and the right next action suggested at the right moment.

Staff designer, alongside a principal designer and a product manager.

Amazon India · Shopping app
UX Leader · 2022 to 2025

7 stores

made visible from the first screen

What does someone need to see to tap a store they didn’t know existed?

The brief said

Improve the value propositions.

What was underneath

People didn’t read copy; they tapped what they could see. So the question became what customers needed to see to choose a store at all.

What changed

Highway: a persistent strip of store tiles that made every Amazon store discoverable at a glance. Then the frameworks and mechanisms to scale it across emerging markets.

Led the design, from first click research to launch.

Amazon India · Fresh
UX Leader · value perception

40 → 80

40→80%

cashback utilisation, with a 1.1% lift in conversion

Customers had the value in hand. They just couldn’t see it.

The brief said

Make customers perceive Fresh’s value.

What was underneath

The value wasn’t being comprehended. Customers knew cashback existed, but not what it was worth or where to use it.

What changed

Made the value legible at the moments that matter. Cashback utilisation rose from 40% to 80%, conversion grew 1.1%, and the solution scaled.

Led UX for the Fresh value experience.

03

Most of design is invisible.

People rarely notice a detail. They notice how it made them feel. I design for the part of the mind that decides before we do.

04

Design the infrastructure. The screens sit on top.

Screens get redesigned every year or two. The names, rules, data and permissions underneath decide what any screen can do, and they outlast every redesign. That is where I spend the most care.
